## Review round 1: Reduce Motion root swap goes through one helper (PreShellMotion)
The dead `OnboardingMotion.rootSwap` constant is gone. AppRoot's transition and animation and OnboardingScreen's step
transition and animation all come from `PreShellMotion`. Under Reduce Motion the root swap and each step change are
instant (no animation, `.identity` transition), as screens.md §1 says ("cross-fade; no animation with Reduce Motion").
The T18 criterion's wording ("swaps the root with a cross-fade only") disagrees with that spec line; the spec wins.
