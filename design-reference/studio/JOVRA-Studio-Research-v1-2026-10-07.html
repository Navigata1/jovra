Findings that survived refutation

Workflow text needs a tennis renderer

Confirmed by the archive manifest and assembly seat. Preserve project lock, keepers, B-roll and edit planning. Replace the still/audio assembly recipe for action video. Contrary evidence: the separate existing editor does contain executable FFmpeg assembly; “no reusable editor exists” was refuted.

Phone recordings need normalization and clock maps

The existing mapped assembler imposes constant-frame clocks, common dimensions/FPS, square pixels and no rotation. Rereading its source confirmed the limits. Preserve its validation; build compatible proxies, original-time mappings and synchronization rather than silently relaxing it.

Private database rows do not secure R2 objects

The signing service needs separate project/object authorization. Direct signed URLs remain usable until expiry, so use short capabilities and checking application links. R2 access documentation.

Record the new backend scope in an ADR

Current ADR 0002 excludes a v1 backend. Jon’s latest request adds backend readiness and optional live cloud. Preserve the old decision and offline behavior; add a new decision documenting the change and activation gates.

Upload time is an early constraint

Planning assumption: 60 minutes per source at 8 Mbps, decimal units, excluding audio/container overhead. These are arithmetic scenarios, not measured footage or invoices.

Formula: Mbps × seconds ÷ 8 ÷ 1,000 = GB. Seven-day storage: GB × 7/30 × $0.015. Six-camera transfer at 100 Mbps is at least 28.8 minutes. Retries and protocol overhead increase the transfer time.

Build in the order that removes uncertainty

S0–S1 · One camera, a complete film

Reconcile scope and contracts while the existing foundation continues. Import an old match, choose keepers, link a lesson and render all five profiles locally.

Planning allocation: first prototype week; estimate only, dependent on foundation and footage. Gate: real source-bound files and full decode/timing/audio evidence.

S2 · Reviewed two-view sync

Add proxy normalization, offset/drift maps, missing coverage and manual review. Keep audio continuous across camera cuts.

Planning allocation: next prototype week; not a release promise. Gate: real two-view anchors plus deliberately difficult synthetic fixtures.

S3 · Six-device field capture

Pairing, independent recordings, honest device acknowledgements and recovery. Capture stays independent of a host or network.

Effort unestimated until two-view/device results. Gate: actual six-phone tests, not simulator success.

S4–S5 · Cloud and measured capability

Provision isolated services, role tests, bounded jobs, usage reservations, private delivery and deletion. Benchmark richer inference before new metric claims. Add native Android and providers by dependency.

Effort unestimated until rendering/model benchmarks and access. Gate: tenant, budget, reliability, device and capability evidence.
