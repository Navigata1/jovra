# JOVRA brand decision

Selected name: **JOVRA Tennis**. Pronunciation: **JOH-vrah**. Short app label: **JOVRA**. Brand initials: **JV**. Tagline: **See your game.**

## Ten name candidates and confidence scores

These are subjective creative confidence scores out of 100: how strongly each name fits this brief, rather than statistical probabilities, survey results, or availability scores. Criteria: distinctive brand character, J/V connection, spoken usability, compactness, and fit for both performance analysis and equipment.

| Rank | Name | Creative confidence | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | JOVRA | 94/100 | J + V, with a rally-inspired ending. Compact, pronounceable, flexible across match analysis and equipment. |
| 2 | JAVEL | 89/100 | Javelin-like precision and forward movement; contains J and V, but has stronger throwing-sport associations. |
| 3 | JOVEN | 86/100 | Friendly, energetic and easy to say; the existing Spanish word meaning young makes search distinction harder. |
| 4 | J-VANT | 84/100 | A nod to advantage with the requested initials; compelling sport meaning but punctuation complicates search. |
| 5 | JET VISION | 81/100 | Fast, direct and clearly about seeing performance; longer and less distinctive. |
| 6 | JOLT VISION | 80/100 | Energetic impact language; sharper and more aggressive than the intended calm studio. |
| 7 | JOURNEY VISION | 78/100 | Strong progress story; lengthy for an icon, header and spoken recommendation. |
| 8 | JUNCTION VISION | 76/100 | Suggests equipment, footage and insight coming together; less obviously athletic. |
| 9 | J-VISION | 74/100 | Literal, readable JV connection; generic structure and weaker standalone identity. |
| 10 | JON VISION | 70/100 | Personal and direct; ties the product tightly to its owner rather than a broader player community. |

The name is a coined brand direction, not an existing dictionary definition. The J and V are deliberate; the final syllable is a creative nod to rally and rhythm. A targeted public web search did not surface an obvious exact-name tennis application; this was a preliminary search, not an App Store catalog, domain or trademark clearance. Availability remains unverified.

## Design system

- Forest: #092A25. Secondary forest: #123C33.
- Ivory: #F4F1E8. Text: #182F29.
- Tennis chartreuse: #D7F56A. Clay flag: #C47752.
- Native system sans-serif, tabular data figures, restrained headings, 8-point spacing.
- Card corners around 22 points. Minimum tap size 44 points.
- Brand: a thoughtful tennis club with precise instruments. Photographic court texture; clean data; personal equipment history.
- Charts must include labels and markers. Status meaning cannot rely on hue.
- Chartreuse uses forest text; ivory uses forest text. Clay is a decorative/flag accent, not the sole body text on ivory.
- Main tabs: Home, Sessions, Record, Insights, Profile.
- Light utility screens and forest focused video/capture screens belong to one system.

Your equipment preferences inspired green/white string contrasts, charcoal racket imagery, precision, and an equipment journal. Manufacturer names appear as user-entered examples; the original mark and UI are independent of them.
