# JOVRA storycraft

Read `references/style-playbook.md` for the chosen style and `../../examples/story-brief.sample.md` for the handoff form. The brief is an editorial proposal, never executable code.

Start from the player’s goal and actual material: what can this film help the player understand, feel or remember? Name that in one sentence. Choose source-linked keepers before adding effects. Give each beat an evidence reference, purpose and transition reason. A missing angle, reaction, quote or lesson stays missing.

Produce `story.brief.md`: profile/version, intended feeling, audience, duration/aspect preference, ordered beats with keeper IDs, primary-audio policy, cue/metric references, music rights, effect intensity, privacy choices, clean alternate, and unresolved decisions. Duration is a target until exact clip lengths are bound. Advance only source-resolvable ideas to the edit-plan stage.

Emotional pacing comes from contrast with a reason: anticipation before a real point, readable action, the actual reaction, then room to breathe. Do not use repetitive intro/outro templates, loud transitions or invented stakes to manufacture importance. Keep faces and locations subject to source permissions and user preference.

Live tennis stays understandable: preserve contact and ball path, retain original-speed examples, cut between points unless a reviewed alternate view explains the same action, and label slow-motion replays. Effects belong around the action, not across the crucial evidence. Music, narration and generated art are optional with rights/consent and disclosure; default to court sound.

Text has a single job per moment. Show provenance on measured feedback, correct dates on progress and the right event-derived score. Use mobile-safe placement, legible holds, caption contrast and a clean reduced-motion treatment. Grade shots for consistency after normalization; never make a contact line disappear or use an aesthetic look as analysis evidence.

Deliver both the intended emotional arc and a source checklist. If the source cannot support the desired story, offer a simpler honest film in the same profile rather than generating the missing experience.
